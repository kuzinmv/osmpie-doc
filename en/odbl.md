## Rights and Obligations of OSMPIE Users

When you use OSMPIE, you're working with data from OpenStreetMap (OSM), which means you're part of a sharing community. This means that any data or results you create using OSMPIE don't belong to you alone—they must be shared with everyone under the same open terms. You're also required to give credit by clearly marking your work as produced using OSMPIE and OSM. Think of it as a "share-alike" arrangement: you benefit from the community's data, and in return, your contributions flow back to the community. For complete details about OSM's copyright and licensing terms, please visit the [OpenStreetMap Copyright page](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright/en).

The ODbL imposes a mandatory share-alike requirement, often referred to as a "copyleft" provision. Specifically:

**3.1 Derivative Database Requirement**: Any database, dataset, or collection that constitutes a "Derivative Database" (as defined in ODbL Section 4.4) produced using OSMPIE must be released under ODbL 1.0 or a compatible license. This obligation is triggered when a user:

- Creates a new database substantially derived from OSM data; or
- Integrates OSM data with other datasets in a manner that creates a derivative work under applicable copyright or sui generis database right laws.

**3.2 Produced Works**: While "Produced Works" (content created using the database but not constituting a derivative database) may be licensed separately, users must still comply with attribution requirements for the underlying data source.
